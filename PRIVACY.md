# Privacy Policy for VeloSonic

Last updated: August 2026

VeloSonic is a Subsonic client designed with privacy in mind.

1. Data Collection: VeloSonic does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, analytics, or telemetry to the developer or any third-party servers.
2. Server Communication: All communication (credentials, music streaming, playlists) occurs directly between your device and the self-hosted Subsonic-compatible server you configure. No intermediate servers are involved.
3. Permissions: Requested permissions (e.g., Internet access, foreground service for audio playback) are used strictly for local app functionality and streaming audio from your server.
4. Contact: If you have questions about this policy, contact [ilazlow@gmx.de].
